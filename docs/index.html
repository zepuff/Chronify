It watches the window, not you

Every five seconds Chronify looks at which window is in front and writes down the app name and the window title. Your rules turn those two strings into a task name, so a day of scattered tabs comes out as four or five honest lines.

A browser tab called TASK-482 · Fix the export dialog becomes Ticket TASK-482. A Zoom window becomes Meetings. Anything on Stack Overflow or the Apple docs becomes Research.

When you step away, the clock stops. Idle time is not billed to anyone, and a video left playing in the foreground no longer counts as work. A real call does, because a call is work.

The rules live in a plain text file you can edit, and they are nine lines long by default.

What came out

The rewriting happens on your Mac. Ollama downloads a model of about 2 GB and understands Ukrainian. Apple Intelligence uses the model already in macOS and downloads nothing. Pick neither and your notes are kept exactly as you wrote them. Chronify installs whichever you choose, so there is nothing to set up by hand.

And then the end of the month arrives

The two jobs everyone puts off are the two Chronify already has the numbers for.

📤 Send hours to PeopleForce

The timesheet

Pushes a day, a chosen day, or the whole month into your PeopleForce timesheet, with the daily status as the comment. It can send the real time ranges the tracker recorded, or one tidy entry per project.

🧾 Invoice

The invoice

Fills your own Word template and writes a PDF next to it. Your details can be read out of an invoice you have already sent, so there is nothing to retype.

📅 Month calendar

The evidence

A heat map of the month in your browser: which days were long, which were quiet, and which are missing from the timesheet entirely.

Nothing leaves this Mac

There is no account, no sign-up, no server of ours. Your hours, notes and statuses live in one folder in your home directory. The only things that ever go out are the hours you explicitly send to PeopleForce.

One folder to back up

~/.work_tracker holds the database, your settings and every saved status. Copy it and you have copied everything.

Upgrades never touch it

config.yaml is created there on the first run and is left alone afterwards, so your edits survive every new version.

Open source

GPL-3.0. Read it, change it, run your own build. The source is on GitHub.

One command

You need macOS and Homebrew. Everything else comes along with it.

brew install zepuff/chronify/chronify
chronify --background

Then look at the top right of your screen for ⏱.
