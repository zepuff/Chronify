It watches the window, not you

Every five seconds Chronify checks which window is in front and turns that into a task name by your own rules. Not "Chrome, 4 hours" but Ticket ABC-123 and Code Review / Git.

⏸ Pause tracking

Off means off

One click and nothing is recorded, for when the work laptop is being used for something that is nobody's business.

idle_threshold_seconds

It asks about the gap

Away for 25 minutes, and when you come back it asks whether that was work. Reading docs counts. Lunch does not. Zoom, Meet and Teams count on their own, because a call is work even with your hands still.

🏷 Project

Hours belong to a project

Switch from the menu bar and the tracker follows. Logged a day against the wrong one? Move that day, or a whole date range, in two clicks.

📋 Plan for today

One item stares back at you

Pin an unfinished item and it sits in the menu bar all day. Whatever you do not finish moves itself to tomorrow.

📅 Month calendar

The month at a glance

A heat map of the month in your browser, each day split by project, with what you logged one click away.

⏰ Remind me at a time

Alarms that give up

Reminders on the days you pick. If the Mac was off and the moment passed by more than two hours, it stays quiet instead of burying you in stale popups.

The status writes itself

During the day you jot notes down however they come out: "fixed the button in safari", "approved the payments PR". In the evening one click turns that into something you can paste into Slack.

Only the first section goes through the model, under tight rules: invent nothing, never mention hours, never name the apps you used. The rest is assembled from what you wrote. 

What was done today:
• Fixed the button rendering in Safari
• Reviewed and approved the payments pull request

What is planned next:
• Finish the timesheet export

Blockers: None

Infrastructure status: 🟢 All systems stable
The model runs on your Mac. Ollama or Apple Intelligence, whichever you pick in Settings, and Chronify installs it for you. There is no OpenAI key, no cloud, no account. Your work notes never leave the laptop.

Timesheets, without the Friday ritual

Hours and the status text go into PeopleForce for a day, for yesterday, or for the whole month at once. Turn auto-push on and it happens after midnight, catching up to a week back if the Mac was off.

You choose what gets sent: the real stretches the tracker recorded, or one entry per project. Days that already have an entry are never overwritten without your say-so.

Invoices in a minute

On the last working day of the month it reminds you and offers a split of hours across projects. Confirm, and a finished .docx with your details, signature and total is open, with a PDF next to it.

Already sent an invoice before? Point Chronify at it and it reads your IBAN, SWIFT, tax number and addresses out of the document, so you never type them again.

Install it in two minutes

Homebrew brings Python and everything else along with it.

brew tap zepuff/chronify
brew trust zepuff/chronify
brew install chronify
chronify --background

Then look at the top right of your screen for ⏱.
