The clock itself

The ⏱ in your menu bar shows how long today has been tracked so far. It is not a stopwatch you have to remember to start: it counts whenever the app is running, a project is selected, and you are actually at the machine.

If you pin something from 📋 Plan for today, that text sits next to the clock all day instead, which is a surprisingly effective way to stop forgetting the one thing you meant to do.

⏸ Pause tracking

Records nothing until you switch it back on

Stops the recording without quitting the app. Use it for the hour that belongs to someone else, or a lunch you would rather not explain later.

While paused the item reads ▶️ Resume tracking, so the menu always tells you which state you are in. The clock in the menu bar keeps the total it had.

🏷 Project

Which project today's hours belong to. Add, edit, move days

The top of this submenu lists your projects with a mark beside the active one. Everything recorded from now on belongs to whichever is selected, so switch here when you switch clients.

  • + Add a projectName, and optionally a PeopleForce id and its own hourly rate. Only the name is required.
  • ✏️ Edit a projectChange any of the three, or delete the project.
  • 🔀 Move a dayA whole day of tracked time, moved from one project to another. For the day you forgot to switch.
  • 📅 Move a date range…The same, over several days at once.
  • Separate tasks & notes per projectOff: one shared list of tasks and notes. On: every project keeps its own.

A project with no PeopleForce id is never sent to PeopleForce. That is the clean way to keep personal or internal work out of the timesheet while still tracking it.

✨ Write today's status

Turns today's notes into text you can paste into Slack

Collects everything you logged under ✅ What I did today, adds the hours from the tracker, appends your blockers as their own section and your infrastructure line if you set one, and produces a finished status.

Depending on who you picked to write it, your lines are either rewritten into proper sentences or kept exactly as typed. Either way the result is saved, so 📝 Status history has it tomorrow.

Statuses are also written for you in the background for days you never pressed this on, as long as there was something to write about.

✅ What I did today

Log each thing you finish. The status is written from these

The habit the whole app is built around. Finish something, open this, write one line. Spelling and grammar do not matter, these lines are raw material.

  • + Log a completed taskOne thing per entry, in whatever words come out.
  • the entriesClick one to edit its text, or to delete it.

The list is today's only, and it empties itself when the date changes. Whatever is in it at the end of the day is what the status gets built from.

💭 Notes to self

Anything worth remembering. No date, stays until you delete it

The difference from ✅ What I did today is that these are not finished work and they have no date. A password hint, a thing to raise at the next one-to-one, the name of the person who owns that service.

They stay until you remove them, and they never appear in a status.

🚧 What is blocking me

Goes into the daily status as its own section

Whatever is in the way. Every blocker here is printed in the daily status under its own heading, which is how the rest of the team finds out without you having to remember to mention it.

  • + Add a blockerWhat is in the way, in one line.
  • ✅ Mark as resolvedRemoves it from future statuses.

With the list empty, the status simply says there are none.

📋 Plan for today

Tick things off. Whatever is left moves to tomorrow

A short list of what you meant to do. Click an item to tick it off. Anything still unticked at the end of the day comes back tomorrow, so a plan never quietly disappears.

  • + Add an itemOne line per thing.
  • 🧹 Clear completedClears out everything already ticked.
  • 📌 Show in the menu barPuts one unfinished item next to the clock, in front of your eyes all day.

⏰ Remind me at a time

Alarms with your own text, on the days you pick

A notification at a time you choose, on the weekdays you choose, with the words you choose. The daily stand-up, the Friday timesheet, the reminder to actually stop working.

  • + New timed reminderThe text, the time, and which days it repeats on.
  • the remindersClick one to edit or remove it.

The sound is set in config.yaml and is Glass by default. Any sound from /System/Library/Sounds works, and an empty value makes reminders silent.

🚦 Infrastructure status

One line added to the status: 🟢 🟡 🔴 or your own wording

For teams whose daily status is expected to say whether things are on fire. Pick one of the ready-made lines, or write your own, and it is appended to every status until you change it.

  • the presets🟢 all stable, 🟡 degraded, 🔴 an incident.
  • ✏️ Custom text…Your own wording, with or without one of the lights in front.

📊 Where today went

Hours per task so far today

Today's recorded time, broken down by task name, with the total at the bottom. This is the quickest way to check that your rules are grouping things sensibly.

If you see raw app names here instead of task names, Screen Recording is off and window titles are invisible. How to fix that.

📆 Another day

The last 30 days: hours, the status, and fixing what was recorded

The same view as above, for any of the last thirty days, plus the ability to correct what was recorded.

  • 📄 Summary and hoursThat day's hours per task, and the status that was saved for it.
  • ✏️ Edit tracked segments…The individual stretches of time as recorded, where you can retitle, merge or remove them.

Edit before you send, not after. Once a day has been pushed to PeopleForce, fixing it here does not change what is already in the timesheet.

📅 Month calendar

Opens a heat map of the month in your browser

A month at a glance: darker days are longer days. Useful at the end of the month for spotting the day you forgot to track, or the day PeopleForce never received.

  • This monthOpens in your default browser.
  • Last monthThe same, one month back.

🧾 Invoice

Make this month's invoice, or read your details from an old one

Fills your own Word template with the month's hours, your rate and your payment details, then writes a PDF beside it. The finished files land in the folder set under Settings → Invoicing, and Chronify shows you that folder when it is done.

  • Create for this monthThe month in progress.
  • Create for last monthThe usual one, since invoices go out after the month ends.
  • 📥 Import details from an existing invoice…Reads your name, tax number, IBAN and the rest out of an invoice you already sent, so there is nothing to retype.
  • Refresh PDF for this monthRebuilds the PDF after you corrected a detail, without changing the numbers.
  • Refresh PDF for last monthThe same, one month back.

The PDF step needs LibreOffice. If it is missing, the .docx is still written and you can convert it yourself: 

brew install --cask libreoffice

Edit the template in Word or LibreOffice only. Pages and TextEdit flatten the invoice table into plain text when they save, and the finished invoice then has no table at all. Chronify checks for this and refuses a template it can see has been flattened.

📤 Send hours to PeopleForce

Sends the hours and the status text into your timesheet

Pushes recorded time into your PeopleForce timesheet, with the daily status as the comment. Only projects that have a PeopleForce id are sent.

  • TodayWhat has been recorded so far today.
  • YesterdayThe one you meant to send this morning.
  • Days this monthEvery day of the current month with its total, so you can send one specific day.
  • 📦 Push every day this monthThe whole month in one go, for when you left it until the last day.
  • Push real time rangesOn: 09:00-10:37 exactly as recorded. Off: one tidy entry per project, starting at the hour set in Settings.
  • Auto-push at 00:00Sends yesterday by itself every night, and catches up to a week back if your Mac was off.

This item needs the company API key and your employee id. Where to find both.

📝 Status history

Every saved status, collected into one file

Every status ever written, newest first, exported into a single Markdown file and opened for you. Handy at review time, or when someone asks what you were doing three weeks ago.

  • Open status historyWrites ~/.work_tracker/history_export.md and opens it.
  • 🛑 Stop generatingCancels a status that is still being written, if the model is taking too long.

⚙️ Settings

PeopleForce key, who writes the status, invoice details, permissions

Four groups of fields plus three actions. Each group carries a mark telling you where it stands: ✅ filled in, ⚠️ started but missing something required, — untouched.

  • PeopleForceThe company API key, your employee id, the hour your day starts.
  • Daily statusWho rewrites your notes, and which language the status comes out in.
  • InvoicingRate, names, client code, and where finished invoices are saved.
  • Payment detailsTax number, IBAN, addresses. The list follows your own template.
  • 📝 Edit invoice template in Word…Opens the template for editing and connects the saved file back.
  • Screen RecordingTells you whether window titles are readable, and reopens the macOS prompt.
  • 🏷 Add a project…The same form as in the Project menu.

Every field is explained on its own page: where each value comes from.

🔄 Reload rules

Re-read config.yaml after editing how windows become tasks

Chronify reads ~/.work_tracker/config.yaml at startup. After you edit the rules that turn window titles into task names, this picks up the change without restarting the app.

It only affects what is recorded from now on. Time already logged keeps the task names it was given. How the rules work.

📁 Show my data folder

Everything the app saves, in ~/.work_tracker. Your backup

Opens the one folder that matters in Finder. The database, your settings, your notes and every saved status are all in there. Copy it and you have copied everything. What each file is.

Quit

Stops tracking until you start Chronify again

Closes the app. Nothing is recorded while it is not running, and nothing is lost either. Start it again with chronify --background, or have it start at login with brew services start chronify.
